# Saint Peter’s Anglican Church — one-page website

Open index.html in any modern browser. No installation, build process, account, or internet connection is needed for the page and its bundled images. External links, email, phone, and directions use the relevant online services/apps.

To publish, upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, and the assets folder together to a static website host. This package has not been published.

## Editing
- Content, worship times, contact details: index.html
- Colours, typography, spacing, responsive layout: styles.css
- Mobile navigation: script.js
- Original badge, anniversary cap mockup, and church photograph: assets/

## Design and content notes
The original conversation supplied a brand board, badge, icon/identity screenshots, Illustrator file, and cap mockup. A website mockup image was not included in the retrievable conversation. This implementation follows the user's specified layout and original visual identity; exact website-mockup matching could not be verified.

The StoryBrand-style sequence is: identity and invitation → visitor desire for connection → church as guide → three next steps → Sunday call to action → missed connection and hoped-for transformation → 75-year credibility and story → practical visit details.

Worship times, address, accessibility, nursery, phone, email, and office hours were checked against https://www.stpetersbirchcove.org/ on September 23, 2026. Anniversary dates follow the supplied brand identity (established 1951; 75 years in 2026). General welcome and narrative copy is newly written for this page.
